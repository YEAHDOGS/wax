# Landing page — apps/web

**Scope:** The marketing landing page. The first Wax surface built, and the one that establishes the visual world the Expo app inherits.

**Visitor mode:** Persuade.

**Audience:** A vinyl collector with a large, growing collection who has already been burned — a limited pressing came and went before they heard about it. They read Discogs daily and know record graphics by heart.

**Job:** Understand in one viewport that Wax sees a pressing the moment it exists and tells them while copies remain, then start tracking three artists for free.

**Action:** Primary — start tracking 3 artists, free. Secondary — read how the watcher works.

**Proof / content:** The mechanism dramatized, not claimed: a live sleeve that re-presses itself with each catch and a running detection counter. The miss is dramatized too — a white-label sleeve under a vermilion SOLD OUT overprint, carrying the collector's own *This Binary Universe* story. Every catalog entry on the page is authored demonstration data, labelled "synthetic" wherever a visitor could read it as a live feed.

**Constraints:**
- No stock photography. Sleeve art is authored — type as the image — which is both period-true and specific to Wax's own catalog.
- Real artist names are used only where the fact is true (the BT reference); no invented figures attach to it.
- Commercial terms are fixed by PRODUCT.md: free tier at 3 artists, $10/month, 30-day trial. Nothing else about price, customers or scale may be asserted.

**Chosen direction:** The Sleeve Program — Reid Miles' Blue Note system. 7th on the ordered grounded list, assigned by concept seed `79d50909`. Staging is the 12-inch square module and the catalog run, chosen over the seed's offered diagonal spine because the square sleeve geometry is the world's own module.

**Memorable moment:** The hero sleeve re-pressing itself — flipping and re-cutting for the next drop with a new catalog number, ink field and type crop, every seven seconds.

**Unresolved:**
- The `$10/month` and free-tier terms are the only commercial claims on the page; anything about user counts or catch rates is currently synthetic and must be replaced with real figures before launch.
- The hero's "Watching 1,284 artists" figure is placeholder and needs either a real number or removal.
- No real testimonials, press or case studies exist yet; none are faked on the page.
