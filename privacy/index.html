Wax — Privacy

We collect nothing here.

This landing page sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and stores nothing about you. There is no account, no form, and no tracker to opt out of, because there is nothing tracking you.

The Wax web app is a separate pre-release prototype. Anything you enter there stays in that prototype's own storage, and its own terms ship with it when it asks you for anything.

Release and marketplace data shown by Wax comes from Discogs, and remains subject to Discogs' own terms.
